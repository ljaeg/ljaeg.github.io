My Work

HRL Laboratories, 11/2021-04/2026

I spent four years working on the quantum program at HRL Laboratories in Malibu, California. I was primarily focused on creating and applying physics-based mathematical models to better understand device failure mechanisms, and leading in-depth studies of the electrical properties of quantum devices. As part of this effort, I was a contributing author on a paper that was published in Nature.

Space Sciences Lab, 05/2019-07/2021

As an undergraduate, I worked at the Space Sciences Lab in Berkeley, California, under Dr. Andrew Westphal, in a group focused on learning about the conditions of the early solar system. I led a project to automate data collection from the NASA Stardust mission with an algorithm based on two-dimensional convolutional neural networks. I detailed these efforts in a first author paper published in the journal Meteoritics and Planetary Science.
